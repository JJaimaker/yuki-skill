# Page patterns

## Weekly or monthly progress

Use `帽句总体判断 -> 条线或项目分区 -> 本期结果 -> 后续节点 -> 卡点与协调`.

- Start top lines with labels such as `本期进展：` and `交付关注：`.
- Put one compact comparison conclusion near the top: current period, previous comparable period, absolute or percentage change, directional judgment, and management implication.
- Put the completed date and result first in each workstream.
- Label future dates `计划上线` or `计划完成`.
- Put `卡点与协调` at the bottom of the same workstream.
- Separate the solution already controlled by the project from management coordination.
- For AI delivery, preserve lifecycle accuracy across data preparation, PRD, prototype, review, technical validation, development, testing, post-test repair, release, and adoption.

## Customer research or customer value

Use `帽句价值结论 -> 产品或场景分区 -> 样本数字 -> 价值主题 -> 客户原声 -> 验证边界`.

- Show sample counts by product or assistant. Do not sum overlapping customer groups into a unique total.
- Compare sample count, recognition, trial, usage, or quantified outcome only when period, scope, definition, and denominator are comparable; otherwise state that the trend is unavailable.
- Use one representative quote per region when space is limited.
- Distinguish `表示认可`, `正在试用`, `已验证`, `已付费`, and `形成量化收益`.
- Use red for weak sample coverage, unresolved customer pain, or unverified outcome.

## Product or business planning

Use `帽句范围与判断 -> 关键数字总账 -> 能力或模块分区 -> 客户价值或业务依据 -> 重点节点 -> 需决策事项`.

- Put total scope at the top, such as capabilities, modules, functions, customers, equipment, or budget.
- Group long lists under a small number of business capability families.
- Explain why each module exists, not only what it is called.
- Mark the must-deliver scope and milestone in bold black; mark gaps or unresolved ownership in red.

## Market, competitor, or opportunity analysis

Use `帽句事实与判断 -> 市场现状 -> 发展机会 -> 行业痛点或竞争差距 -> 选择与行动`.

Use a small native chart, evidence excerpt, or image only when it directly supports the judgment. Avoid decorative photos that occupy decision space.

## Compression method

1. Keep one sentence per conclusion.
2. Keep two or three supporting facts under it.
3. Move implementation detail to notes or appendix.
4. Retain dates, numbers, owners, equipment models, customer roles, dependencies, and exceptions.
5. Delete repeated background, generic descriptions, and routine activity that does not change status.
