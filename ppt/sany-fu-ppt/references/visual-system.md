# Visual system

## Character

Create an industrial operating brief: authoritative, practical, evidence-rich, and easy to scan. Avoid a marketing-poster or app-dashboard appearance.

## Canvas and header

- Use a white 16:9 canvas unless a supplied deck fixes another size.
- Place a short red vertical rule at the far left of the title.
- Use a large black or dark-navy Chinese title.
- Keep the SANY logo at the upper right when supplied or already present.
- Use a thin red horizontal rule below the title.

## Top summary lines

- Place one to three full-width lines directly under the title.
- Start each line with a short bold black label such as `本期进展：`, `价值判断：`, or `重点关注：`.
- Apply blue, red, and bold black to phrases inside the sentence instead of using colored boxes.
- Communicate the page conclusion before the audience reads the body.

## Body structure

- Use two to four aligned regions based on the real business structure.
- Give every region a medium-blue title bar with white bold text.
- Use a light gray or white body field with thin separators.
- Use `1.`, `2.`, `3.` numbered mini-headings for conclusions.
- Use a heavier Chinese sans-serif for titles and numbered conclusions.
- Use a Chinese Kai-style font for short explanations and authentic customer voice when available.
- Keep text left aligned, column widths consistent, and baselines strict.

## Data and evidence

- Use editable native tables for counts and comparisons.
- Use editable native charts only when a relationship is clearer visually than in a short sentence.
- Use real project or customer images only when supplied and relevant. Never fabricate documentary evidence.
- Keep citations, scope notes, and counting definitions in a small footer or speaker notes.

## Density and emphasis

- Preserve useful density while keeping a dominant top-to-bottom reading path.
- Reduce copy before shrinking body text.
- Avoid excessive pills, rounded cards, icons, gradients, shadows, and decorative illustrations.
- Emphasize only conclusions, numbers, milestones, exceptions, and decisions.

## Recommended palette

- SANY blue: `#0074B9`
- dark text: `#181818`
- critical red: `#BF191F`
- light blue: `#EAF2FA`
- panel gray: `#F5F6F7`
- separator gray: `#D5D9DF`

Status colors can additionally use green for healthy and orange for recoverable risk, but the core President Fu text semantics remain blue for progress, red for problems, and bold black for decisions.
